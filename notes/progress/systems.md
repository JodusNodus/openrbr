# Gameplay systems inventory (1.02 → Rust)

Every gameplay system and flow of Richard Burns Rally 1.02, where it stands in Rust, and what is left. Updated 2026-10-17 on integration `6a4875d4`; statuses audited 2026-10-18 on `f19b9ac7` (`lead/reports/audit.md`); from the notes, the exe's RTTI and strings, the retail data, the Rust crates and the worker reports in `lead/reports/`.
Menus as a UI are out of scope (the launcher replaces them); the rules they configure are in.

Columns:
- **Original**: the code (1.02 exe VAs unless marked HH or LM) and the data.
- **Rust**: *done* (where), *partial* (what exists and what is missing), *not started*, *out of scope (user)* (the user excluded it), *user's own work* (force feedback and NGP physics), or *ours (user)* (the user's own additions to 1.02).
- **Check**: *bit-exact* (equal to the original per step or per call: recordings, snapshots, vectors), *compared*
  (checked against the original's state or data, not bit for bit every step), *presentation* (looks or sounds right,
  reviewed by eye or ear), *none* (not checked yet).
- **Size**: S (under one 3 h task), M (1-2 tasks), L (3+ tasks). Dependencies follow.
- **G** marks gameplay-critical rows (they change times, penalties, results or the car's motion); the rest are
  presentation.

"Untraced" means the notes and decompile do not settle it; a row with it needs tracing before porting.

## 3. Game modes and their flows

The exe has these modes (RTTI and string keys): quick rally (`QUICK_RALLY`, `CQR*`), rally and championship season
(`RALLY_SEASON`, `CSeason*`, `NSeason::`, `CAISeason`), rally school (`RALLY_SCHOOL`, `CRS*`, `CSchool*`), the Richard
Burns Challenge (`RB_CHALLENGE`, `CRBC*`), and hot seat (`HOT_STAGE`, `HOT_RALLY`, `HOT_SEASON`, `CMP*`, `CHotSeat*`).
There is no separate time-trial mode: `S_YARD_TRIAL` and `S_STAGE_TRIAL` are practice drives on the season's page
between rallies (0x19, the player's kind-11 event; maps 72 and 71, flow mode 9), and 1.02 keeps no result of them
(season-2). DirectPlay is imported, but every `CMP*` class found is hot seat;
network play is untraced.
